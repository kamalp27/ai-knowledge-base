Principles of Quantum Computing and Physical Realizations

Section 1: Qubit Representations and Superposition
Unlike classical digital bits constrained to binary state 0 or state 1, quantum bits (qubits) exist as linear superpositions represented mathematically on the Bloch sphere by the state vector |ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + β|1⟩, where complex probability amplitudes satisfy |α|^2 + |β|^2 = 1. When measured in the computational basis, a qubit collapses stochastically to |0⟩ with probability |α|^2 or |1⟩ with probability |β|^2.

Section 2: Quantum Entanglement and Bell States
Entanglement is a non-local quantum correlation between two or more qubits where the composite quantum state cannot be decomposed into product states of individual subsystems. Maximally entangled two-qubit pairs are known as Bell states: |Φ+⟩ = (|00⟩ + |11⟩) / √2. Entanglement provides the quantum speedup mechanism powering quantum key distribution (QKD) and superdense coding protocols.

Section 3: Physical Qubit Modalities and Cryogenics
Superconducting transmon circuits utilize Josephson junctions acting as non-linear inductors to isolate the lower two energy states. Because thermal energy (k_B * T) would instantly excite the qubits and induce thermal decoherence, superconducting quantum processors must operate inside multi-stage dilution refrigerators maintained at base temperatures of approximately 10 to 15 millikelvin (-273.14 degrees Celsius).

Section 4: Decoherence Times and Quantum Error Correction
Qubit reliability is fundamentally limited by two characteristic relaxation timescales:
- T1 Relaxation Time: The longitudinal energy relaxation duration required for an excited state |1⟩ to decay spontaneously into the ground state |0⟩.
- T2 Dephasing Time: The transverse phase coherence duration over which superposition relative phase information is corrupted by magnetic and electromagnetic noise.
Surface codes utilize topological arrangements of physical data qubits and syndrome measurement ancilla qubits to detect bit-flip and phase-flip errors without collapsing the underlying encoded logical qubit.
